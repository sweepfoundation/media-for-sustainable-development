MEDIA FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Sustainable Development Media Group (SDMG)

"Telling Africa's Stories. Finding Solutions. Advancing Sustainable Development."

The Sustainable Development Media Group (SDMG) is being established as a continental platform dedicated to strengthening the role of the media in advancing sustainable development across Africa, with a particular focus on Solutions Journalism, development reporting, public-interest storytelling, evidence-based reporting and accountability.

As part of the process of defining the Group's programmes, services and strategic priorities, we are undertaking a broad stakeholder consultation to better understand the needs, interests and expectations of journalists, media organisations, development practitioners, civil society organisations, researchers and other relevant stakeholders across Africa.

The consultation will help shape SDMG into a practical, accessible and responsive platform that reflects the realities, opportunities and challenges facing Africa's media and development community.

KEY AREAS OF CONSULTATION

Among the key areas we seek to explore are:

  • Capacity building and professional development for journalists
  • Solutions Journalism and evidence-based reporting
  • The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Africa's development priorities
  • Climate change, environmental and sustainability journalism
  • Investigative and data-driven journalism
  • Access to development information, research and data
  • Digital storytelling, documentaries and multimedia journalism
  • Fellowships, grants and media partnership opportunities
  • Cross-border reporting, collaboration and knowledge exchange
  • Innovative approaches to strengthening development communication across Africa

STRATEGIC INITIATIVES

The findings and recommendations from this consultation will inform the development of SDMG's strategic programmes and institutional framework, including:

  1. SDMG Strategic Plan – providing the strategic direction, priorities and development roadmap for the Group.
  2. Membership Framework – establishing an inclusive network of journalists, media professionals, development practitioners, organisations and other stakeholders committed to sustainable development communication.
  3. Solutions Journalism Academy – providing structured training, mentorship and professional development opportunities focused on Solutions Journalism and evidence-based reporting.
  4. Sustainable Development Media Forum – a regular platform bringing together media professionals, development experts, policymakers, researchers, civil society, private-sector actors and other stakeholders to discuss development challenges, share solutions and strengthen collaboration.
  5. Africa Media for Development Forum – a continental platform for dialogue, knowledge exchange and collaboration on the role of media in advancing Africa's development priorities.
  6. Establishment of an Advisory Board – bringing together experienced media professionals, development experts, academics, researchers and other relevant stakeholders to provide strategic guidance, technical advice and institutional support to SDMG.
  7. Other Flagship Programmes and Partnerships – including fellowships, grants, research initiatives, cross-border collaborations, media partnerships and innovative platforms that advance sustainable development journalism across Africa.

INVITATION TO PARTICIPATE

We warmly invite journalists, editors, broadcasters, publishers, development organisations, civil society groups, academics, researchers, government institutions, private-sector organisations and other interested stakeholders across Africa to participate in this consultation and share their perspectives.

We particularly welcome your ideas, recommendations, experiences and suggestions on how SDMG can best serve the African media and development community and contribute meaningfully to sustainable development across the continent.

Your views will help us build a platform that is inclusive, credible, practical and responsive to the evolving needs of Africa's media and development ecosystem.

HOW TO PARTICIPATE

You are invited to submit your views, ideas and recommendations directly to SDMG. We also encourage you to share this consultation with individuals and organisations that may have an interest in strengthening Solutions Journalism, sustainable development reporting and development communication across Africa.
